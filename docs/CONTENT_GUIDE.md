# Content Guide

## Principle
The personal content is the reason this experience exists. Do not invent it, over-polish it into generic compliments, or fill missing sections with fabricated memories.

## Page 2 — Through My Eyes
Build the content from real observations and shared experiences. Possible ingredients:
- What makes this person distinct.
- Small habits, quirks, and details the creator notices.
- Qualities the creator appreciates.
- Shared memories, conversations, and inside jokes.
- The person's place in the creator's life.

These are ingredients, not mandatory headings. Let the actual material determine the structure.

## Page 3 — Ending
The message should feel sincere and specific:
- What the person means to the creator.
- What the creator wants them to carry into the new year.
- Personal hopes and wishes.
- A natural closing line.

Avoid generic greeting-card language unless it genuinely sounds like the creator.

## Recipient data
Keep recipient-specific content in separate data/config files. Use neutral placeholders until actual content is supplied. Do not put personal messages directly into reusable UI components.
