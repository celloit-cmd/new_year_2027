# Creative Direction

## Overall contrast
The opening is cinematic and celestial. Once the recipient enters their personal experience, the design becomes quiet, minimal, and intimate. The visual spectacle must not compete with the message.

## Page 1 — Gateway
- Visual style: celestial, deep-space atmosphere.
- Begin with a dark starfield and a prominent star-shaped START control.
- On click, a large burst of stars fills the screen.
- After a short beat, particles spiral/converge and physically form “HAPPY NEW YEAR 2027”.
- Reveal the CTA: “View Your Journey From My POV”.
- Show an invitation-code prompt.
- A valid code initiates a portal transition into the selected personal journey.
- The portal should feel like a transition in the experience, not a loading screen.

## Page 2 — Through My Eyes
- Plain, simple, dark background: near-black or midnight navy.
- A subtle animated starfield may continue from Page 1 for continuity.
- Use a clean page layout for the message. No console/terminal treatment.
- No mythological, fantasy-landscape, or overly cinematic presentation.
- Use readable typography, generous whitespace, and restrained accents.
- Subtle text reveals are acceptable; avoid effects that obstruct reading.
- Message is the visual and emotional focus. Do not put all content in one oversized card by default.

## Page 3 — Ending
- Individualized for each recipient.
- Quieter and more intimate than the opening.
- A personal letter/message and New Year wish are the emotional payoff.
- Exact layout and visual treatment remain open until content is known.

## Page 4 — Your Turn (optional)
- Not yet decided.
- If implemented, it must be optional and playful/welcoming, never emotionally pressuring.
- Do not require a response to finish the experience.

## Personalization
The two journeys share reusable structure but have distinct content and subtle visual identity. Avoid stereotypes and do not assign aesthetics based on gender. Derive each identity from real details supplied by the creator.

## Motion and sound
- Motion should support pacing and emotion, not exist merely to show off.
- Keep Page 2 motion particularly restrained.
- Sound is undecided; do not add autoplay audio. If sound is later added, provide an obvious control and keep the experience usable when muted.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
